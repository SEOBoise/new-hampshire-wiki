Privacy Policy

Effective and last reviewed September 29, 2026.

New Hampshire Wiki is a static informational website. It does not provide user accounts, comments, checkout, newsletter registration, advertising profiles, or a direct-contact form.

Information you provide

The site does not ask visitors to submit names, email addresses, postal addresses, payment details, or account credentials. Page search runs in the visitor's browser and is not submitted to New Hampshire Wiki.

Routine technical data

Hosting, content-delivery, domain-name, and security providers may process routine request data needed to deliver and protect the site. This may include an IP address, request time, requested URL, browser or device information, referring page, and security signals. Those providers handle data under their own policies and operational requirements.

Cookies and analytics

The published site does not intentionally set first-party advertising or analytics cookies and does not include an advertising or behavioral-tracking tag. Infrastructure providers may use essential security or network mechanisms when serving requests.

External links

The site includes links to third-party pages. A third party's privacy practices apply after a visitor follows its link. New Hampshire Wiki does not control those sites or their data handling.

Policy changes

This policy may change when the site or its technical operation changes. The effective date on this page identifies the current published version.

Content reviewed September 29, 2026. Corrections may be submitted through the site owner.
